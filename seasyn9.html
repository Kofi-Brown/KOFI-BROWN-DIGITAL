SEASYN9 BISTRO

Good Food.
Great Vibes.

Restaurant website concept by Kofi Brown Digital.

THE CHALLENGE

Turn a restaurant into a digital experience.

The goal was more than displaying a menu: the website needed to make the brand appealing, keep information easy to find and create a natural path toward ordering or contacting the restaurant.

THE APPROACH
  • Strong food-led visual hierarchy
  • Mobile-first menu browsing
  • Clear calls to action
  • Ordering-focused customer flow
  • Responsive layouts across screen sizes
THE RESULT

A stronger foundation for online growth.

Brand

Premium visual identity carried through the experience.

Experience

Simple navigation and menu presentation.

Growth

Ready to expand into payments, analytics and promotions.
